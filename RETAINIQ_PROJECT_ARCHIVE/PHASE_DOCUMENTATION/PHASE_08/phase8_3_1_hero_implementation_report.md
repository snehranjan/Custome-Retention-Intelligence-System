## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORRECTIONS COMPLETED

Following an in-depth visual review of browser screenshots across desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile viewports, targeted refinements were executed on the RetainIQ Animated Hero Section to guarantee complete technical accuracy, academic integrity, and responsive perfection without altering the approved design direction, branding, or color palette.

### Summary of Targeted Corrections:
1. **Dashboard Status & Live Indicators:**
   - Replaced potentially misleading live-status wording `RETENTION OVERVIEW` and green pulsing dot with `PREVIEW MODE` accompanied by a static electric-blue indicator dot.
   - Preserved `ILLUSTRATIVE PREVIEW` badge prominently in the header bar.
   - Eliminated any implication of a live, real-time backend analytics stream.
2. **Probability & Calibration Terminology:**
   - Corrected `74.2% Calibrated Churn Probability` to `74.2% Illustrative Churn Risk Score`.
   - Removed all unsupported claims of "calibrated" probabilities across user-facing text and internal component documentation.
   - Updated callout footnote to: *"Illustrative demonstration based on 43 customer behavior features."*
3. **Official Project Customer Segment Personas:**
   - Replaced abbreviated placeholder strings with the exact official RFM segment definitions:
     1. `Champions & High-Value Loyalists` (Cluster 0)
     2. `Steady Core Repeaters` (Cluster 3)
     3. `At-Risk / Lapsed Low-Value Customers` (Cluster 1)
   - Updated layout in `CustomerRiskList.jsx` to ensure long persona labels wrap naturally and display with 100% legibility on all viewport widths without truncation.
4. **Churn Risk Distribution Chart Layout & Label Fix:**
   - Removed CSS `truncate` from legend labels.
   - Restructured legend typography (`text-[10px] sm:text-[11px] leading-tight break-words`) and column spacing (`gap-1 sm:gap-2`) so all three categories display fully without ellipsis:
     - `Low Risk`
     - `Moderate Risk` (previously truncated to `Modera...`)
     - `High Risk` (previously truncated to `High Ri...`)
   - Replaced header threshold note with `Threshold: τ = 0.50` (zero usage of the term "calibration").
5. **KPI Card Terminology & Clarity:**
   - Changed `Across 5 RFM clusters` to `Across 5 customer segments`.
   - Changed `Target 0.50` to `Threshold: 0.50` to clarify that $0.50$ is the locked ML decision threshold ($\tau$), not an organizational business target.
   - Refined metric card subtitles to `Illustrative cohort dataset` and `Risk Score ≥ 0.50`.
   - Enabled flexible badge wrapping (`flex-wrap gap-1.5`) in `HeroKpiCard.jsx` to eliminate horizontal crowding on laptop viewports ($1024$px).
6. **Demonstration Retention Recommendation:**
   - Explicitly relabeled action callout to `ILLUSTRATIVE RETENTION ACTION (DEMONSTRATION)`.
   - Clarified that the ₹150 apology credit and Express Courier dispatch recommendation is a demonstration example, not generated by an active inference pipeline.
7. **Hero Content Solid Rendering:**
   - Converted body paragraph, CTA button row, and trust indicator bar in `HeroContent.jsx` to standard HTML wrappers, eliminating any initial opacity lag in headless browsers or reduced-motion environments.
